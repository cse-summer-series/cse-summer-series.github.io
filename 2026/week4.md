Lightning data structures quiz for you. Consider these implementations of
symmetric tree:

1. Do an in-order traversal, check if the resulting list is the same forwards
   and backwards
2. Check `isSameTree(root.left, root.right)`
3. Create a mirrored copy of the tree and check if the mirrored copy is
   identical to the original

Which ones work, which ones don't? Most people get this wrong, so just take a
guess — clicking takes you straight to the answer.

- [All of them are correct](https://cse-summer-series.github.io/2026/week4-page.html#q-all)
- [Just 1 is correct](https://cse-summer-series.github.io/2026/week4-page.html#q-just1)
- [Just 2 is correct](https://cse-summer-series.github.io/2026/week4-page.html#q-just2)
- [Just 3 is correct](https://cse-summer-series.github.io/2026/week4-page.html#q-just3)
- [Two of them are right, one of them is wrong](https://cse-summer-series.github.io/2026/week4-page.html#q-two)
- [None of them are correct](https://cse-summer-series.github.io/2026/week4-page.html#q-none)
